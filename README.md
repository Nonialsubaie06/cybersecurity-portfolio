## Academic Research

### Digital Forensics
Research on digital evidence, evidence admissibility, chain of custody, and digital forensic practices.

### Digital Watermarking & Fingerprinting
Research on combining digital watermarking, fingerprinting, and cryptographic techniques for copyright protection.

### Phishing Email Detection
Research on machine-learning-based phishing email detection using linguistic, URL, header, psychological, and metadata features.

### Operating System Security
Research on malware categories, detection techniques, prevention mechanisms, and adaptive security frameworks for operating systems.

### Insider Threat Detection
Research on improving insider threat detection through data preprocessing and machine-learning-based behavioral analysis.

### Mathematical Models for Bot Detection
Research applying mathematical and probabilistic models to distinguish bots from legitimate users in web services.
